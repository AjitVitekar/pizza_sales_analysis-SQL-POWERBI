## 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard

**SQL + Power BI | End-to-End Analytics Project**

---

## 📌 About This Project

I developed this **Pizza Sales Analysis Dashboard** as a complete **end-to-end data analytics project**, leveraging my hands-on experience in **SQL and Power BI**.

The goal was not just to create visuals, but to **analyze business performance from raw transactional data**, derive meaningful KPIs using SQL, and present those insights through a **professional, multi-page Power BI dashboard** with smooth navigation.

This project closely reflects how **real-world BI dashboards** are built and used by business stakeholders.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

While working on this project, I focused on solving key business questions such as:

* What is the overall revenue and order performance?
* Which pizzas are driving maximum revenue and volume?
* Which products are underperforming and need attention?
* How do orders vary by **day** and **month**?
* Which pizza categories and sizes contribute the most to sales?

The dashboard is designed to make these insights **easy to understand, interactive, and decision-ready**.

I worked with a **sample pizza sales dataset** containing:

* Order dates and order IDs
* Pizza names, categories, and sizes
* Quantity sold
* Revenue per transaction

* **Total Orders:** 21,350

* **Average Pizzas per Order:** 2.32

## 🔄 My End-to-End Approach

1. Understood the business requirements and data structure
2. Imported raw data into SQL
3. Wrote optimized SQL queries for KPIs and analysis
4. Analyzed:

   * Daily and monthly order trends
   * Sales contribution by category and size
   * Top 5 and bottom 5 pizzas
5. Connected SQL database to Power BI
6. Cleaned and shaped data using Power Query
7. Built KPIs and visuals using DAX
8. Designed a **two-page interactive dashboard**
9. Implemented **navigation buttons** for better user experience

## 📊 Dashboard Pages Overview

### 🏠 Home Page – Sales Overview

On the Home page, I provide a **high-level overview of sales performance**, including:

* Daily trend of total orders
* Monthly trend of total orders
* % of sales by pizza category

### ⭐ Best & Worst Sellers Page

<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/AjitVitekar/pizza_sales_analysis-SQL-POWERBI/main/Snapshot%20of%20the%20Pizza_Sales_Report(Best%20%26%20Worst%20Sellers).png" width="900">

* Top 5 pizzas by **Revenue**
* Top 5 pizzas by **Quantity Sold**
* Top 5 pizzas by **Total Orders**

**Key insights:**

* *The Thai Chicken Pizza* generates the highest revenue
